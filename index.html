§ 01

About me

I am a physics researcher from Srinagar, Kashmir, with an MSc from Jamia Millia Islamia and a BSc (Hons) in Physics from the University of Delhi. My research experience spans optical instrumentation at the Inter-University Accelerator Centre (IUAC), Delhi, and experimental condensed matter physics at IIITDM Kurnool. I am interested in condensed matter physics, quantum materials and precision instrumentation, and I am looking for research and job opportunities where I can contribute to experimental physics. Feel free to get in touch.

Research & projects

Lab work, computation and teaching.

  1. Jun – Sep 2025
    IIITDM Kurnool · Research Project

    Cobalt-decorated cobalt ferrite nanoparticles for EMI shielding

    Synthesized cobalt-decorated cobalt ferrite nanoparticles by co-precipitation for EMI shielding (supervisor: Dr. P. V. Madduri). Crystallinity confirmed by XRD and TEM; analysis with OriginLab and FullProf refinement.

    Shielding effectiveness > 40 dB
    XRDTEMFullProfOriginLab
  2. Jan – Jun 2025
    IUAC Delhi · Research Intern

    Helium DC-discharge UV light source for a Penning trap

    Designed and fabricated a helium DC-discharge UV light source for measuring the zero-point energy of a quantum harmonic oscillator in a Penning trap. Built an AutoCAD-based prototype and improved precision control for spectroscopic applications.

    InstrumentationAutoCADSpectroscopy

    Jamia Millia Islamia

    Physics of the boomerang

    Studied the aerodynamics and stability of boomerang flight using computational and experimental methods, analysing trajectories, lift and torque to explain gyroscopic stability.

About me

I am a physics researcher from Srinagar, Kashmir, with an MSc from Jamia Millia Islamia and a BSc (Hons) in Physics from the University of Delhi. My research experience spans optical instrumentation at the Inter-University Accelerator Centre (IUAC), Delhi, and experimental condensed matter physics at IIITDM Kurnool.

I am interested in condensed matter physics, quantum materials and precision instrumentation, and I am looking for research and job opportunities where I can contribute to experimental physics. Feel free to get in touch.

Cobalt-decorated cobalt ferrite nanoparticles for EMI shielding

Helium DC-discharge UV light source for a Penning trap

Designed and fabricated a helium DC-discharge UV light source for measuring the zero-point energy of a quantum harmonic oscillator in a Penning trap. Built an AutoCAD-based prototype and improved precision control for spectroscopic applications.
